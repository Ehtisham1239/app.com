Questions

Answers before you begin

What US parents usually ask before booking a trial.

Is the trial really free?

Yes. You get a 3-day free trial and no card is needed. The Weekend plan is not part of the free trial.

How do online classes work?

Classes are live video lessons with one teacher and one student. You need a laptop, tablet or phone with a camera, a stable internet connection and a quiet place to sit.

Can my daughter or I learn with a female teacher?

You can share a teacher preference when booking. Admissions confirms whether a suitable teacher is available for your course, language and requested time before enrolment.

What times are classes available in the US?

Share your city, time zone and preferred local times on the form. Admissions confirms whether a suitable teacher and time are available before enrolment.

What age can my child start?

Children can start Noorani Qaida from about 4½ years old. Adults are welcome in every course.

How long is each class?

Our monthly packages are built on 30-minute classes, two to four times a week.

Is there a discount for siblings?

Yes. Additional siblings receive 15% off.

Does sending the form reserve a teacher?

Not yet. Your coordinator first confirms the teacher and the time with you, then the trial begins.

What happens after the trial?

If you are happy, choose a package and weekly schedule and regular classes continue with your teacher. If it is not the right fit, there is nothing to pay.
