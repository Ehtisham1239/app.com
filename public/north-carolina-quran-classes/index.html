Are Quran classes online for families in North Carolina?

Yes. Online Quran College teaches live one-to-one lessons online. There is no local classroom or branch; students learn from home using a phone, tablet or computer.

Which courses can children and adults study?

Learners can ask about Noorani Qaida, Quran reading, Tajweed, Hifz, Arabic and Islamic Studies. Admissions helps confirm the suitable course and teacher.

Can children in Charlotte, Raleigh or Durham join the same online programme?

Yes. Share your city, time zone and preferred days on the free-trial form. Admissions confirms teacher and time availability before enrolment.

Is there a free trial?

OQC offers a 3-day free trial with no card needed. The Weekend plan is excluded from the free trial.

Does OQC have a physical office in North Carolina?

No. OQC is an online-only Quran education provider. Classes are delivered online, and the state page helps families understand the courses and request a suitable schedule.
