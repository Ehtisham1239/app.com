How online Quran classes work

Tell us what you need

Choose the learner’s age and course, then share your city, time zone and preferred days on the free-trial form.

Confirm the arrangements

Admissions checks teacher and time availability with you. Sending the form does not reserve a teacher or class slot.

Begin the trial

Meet your teacher online, practise together and decide whether the learning approach is right for your family.

Plan a trial in Central Time

For learners in Kansas City, St. Louis or Springfield, start with the time shown on the learner’s local clock.

For an adult learner with rotating shifts, give Admissions one Central Time option for each shift pattern and ask whether a recurring arrangement is available. If you are returning to recitation after a break, ask to start with a short reading assessment.

Questions about online Quran classes in Missouri

Compare monthly packages in USD or view the teaching team. You can request a teacher preference on the trial form and confirm availability with Admissions.

Are Quran classes online for families in Missouri?

Yes. Online Quran College teaches live one-to-one lessons online. There is no local classroom or branch; students learn from home using a phone, tablet or computer.

Which courses can children and adults study?

Learners can ask about Noorani Qaida, Quran reading, Tajweed, Hifz, Arabic and Islamic Studies. Admissions helps confirm the suitable course and teacher.

Do you teach students in both Kansas City and St. Louis?

Yes. Share your city, time zone and preferred days on the free-trial form. Admissions confirms teacher and time availability before enrolment.

Is there a free trial?

OQC offers a 3-day free trial with no card needed. The Weekend plan is excluded from the free trial.

Does OQC have a physical office in Missouri?

No. OQC is an online-only Quran education provider. Classes are delivered online, and the state page helps families understand the courses and request a suitable schedule.
