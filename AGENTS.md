## Owner decisions — do not change without the owner's written approval

- **Analytics (26 Oct 2026; ad part amended 18 Nov 2026):** no analytics banner or "Analytics preferences" button. GA4 runs cookieless with `analytics_storage: "granted"` so hits appear in GA4 reports; Google signals stay off. GA4 ignores `client_storage: "none"`, so `shieldAnalyticsCookies` keeps `_ga*`/`_gid`/`_gat` cookies in page memory only (nothing written to the device) and each page load sends a random `client_id`. Ad consent follows the 18 Nov 2026 decision below. Do not switch analytics storage to "denied" (reports go empty), do not remove the cookie shield, and do not bring back the banner. `tests/analytics.test.mjs` pins this.
- **Google Tag Manager (26 Oct 2026):** allowed and active. Container `GTM-W7CMZS53` (tech.iqratech@gmail.com GTM account "Online Quran College", published with owner approval) owns GA4: Google tag `G-57TGPTDNT4` plus page_view, generate_lead, contact_click and web-vitals GA4 events from the site's `oqc_*` dataLayer events, so the direct gtag script is not loaded — loading both double-counts. The site pushes `set` with the page `client_id` and the consent state before the container loads; the cookie shield applies in both modes. `16203239528` is the GA4 web data stream ID, not a GTM ID. Inside the container add no cookie-setting or ad tags without owner approval.
- **Google Ads conversion tracking (18 Nov 2026, CEO, written answers in chat):**
  - *Container:* `GTM-W7CMZS53` stays the only container. `GTM-52PTXDRF` (holds only the Ads Google tag) is not installed: two containers would load AW-16564445897 twice. The CMS head-code validator (`src/lib/custom-code.js`) rejects it and any direct `gtag/js?id=AW-` snippet.
  - *Ad consent:* ad measurement cookies are allowed for every visitor, with no banner: consent default is all denied, then the update grants `analytics_storage`, `ad_storage` and `ad_user_data`; `ad_personalization` stays denied and `allow_ad_personalization_signals`/`allow_google_signals` stay false. The Google Ads click cookies (`_gcl_*`) are stored normally so a lead can be attributed on the thank-you page. On a landing URL with `gclid`/`gbraid`/`wbraid` the tags start at once (no interaction delay) so the click ID is stored before navigation. Chosen with the UK/EU cookie-consent risk of no banner flagged to the owner; the privacy policy must describe these cookies.
  - *Lead conversions:* counted only on the thank-you page after a database-confirmed save, once per lead reference (`src/lib/conversion-events.js`). dataLayer events: `free_trial_submission` (free trial and package enquiries on `/free-trial`, and the USA landing page; primary) and `contact_form_submission` (contact subjects Course enquiry, Fees and payment, Class timings, Teacher request; secondary). Each carries `form_id`, `form_type`, `lead_type`, `market`, `lead_id` and `transaction_id` (= the TR-/MSG- reference) and no personal data. Careers, Existing student support, Partnership and Other messages, failed or spam submissions, refreshes, revisits and direct visits never count. No pre-redirect `oqc_generate_lead` event is sent. `tests/conversion-events.test.mjs`, `tests/analytics.test.mjs` and `tests/usa-landing.test.mjs` pin this.
