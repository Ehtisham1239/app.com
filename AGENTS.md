## Owner decisions — do not change without the owner's written approval

- **Analytics (26 Oct 2026):** no analytics banner or "Analytics preferences" button. GA4 runs cookieless with `analytics_storage: "granted"` so hits appear in GA4 reports; `ad_storage`, `ad_user_data` and `ad_personalization` stay denied and Google signals stay off. GA4 ignores `client_storage: "none"`, so `shieldAnalyticsCookies` keeps `_ga*`/`_gid`/`_gat`/`_gcl*` cookies in page memory only (nothing written to the device) and each page load sends a random `client_id`. Do not switch analytics storage to "denied" (reports go empty), do not remove the cookie shield, and do not bring back the banner. `tests/analytics.test.mjs` pins this.
- **Google Tag Manager (26 Oct 2026):** allowed and active. Container `GTM-W7CMZS53` (tech.iqratech@gmail.com GTM account "Online Quran College", published with owner approval) owns GA4: Google tag `G-57TGPTDNT4` plus page_view, generate_lead, contact_click and web-vitals GA4 events from the site's `oqc_*` dataLayer events, so the direct gtag script is not loaded — loading both double-counts. The site pushes `set` with the page `client_id` and the denied ad consent before the container loads; the cookie shield applies in both modes. `16203239528` is the GA4 web data stream ID, not a GTM ID. Inside the container add no cookie-setting or ad tags without owner approval.
- **Free trial (26 Oct 2026):** 3 days, Weekend plan excluded.
- **Brand colours (26 Oct 2026):** logo green (`#0C6C0C` / `#249C0C`), deep green `#0D4A1C`, soft gold accents.
- **Official profiles (9 Nov 2026, owner):** OQC controls the footer's Facebook, Instagram and X accounts, so Organization `sameAs` lists those exact URLs plus Trustpilot (`organizationIdentityProfiles` in `src/lib/seo.js`, pinned by `tests/seo.test.mjs`). Any other URL in `site.social` stays an ordinary link.
- **Display currencies (8 Nov 2026, owner):** prices are shown only in GBP, USD, AUD and AED (`DISPLAY_CURRENCIES` in `src/lib/fx.js`); every other country and location is quoted in USD, and the admin price list offers only these four. The home hero has no gold "3-day free trial" badge. `tests/fx.test.mjs`, `tests/location-schedule.test.mjs` and `tests/public-accessibility-regression.test.mjs` pin this; it narrows the 29 Oct decision below.
- **Local-currency prices (29 Oct 2026):** show prices converted live into the visitor's currency. The visitor's country (Vercel `x-vercel-ip-country`, via `/api/geo`, no cookie) preselects the currency on `/pricing`; on hosts without that header (Hostinger, 8 Nov 2026) the browser time zone decides (`currencyForTimeZone` in `src/lib/fx.js`, nothing sent anywhere); daily USD rates come from ExchangeRate-API (`src/lib/fx.js`, attributed on the page). An approved price saved in the CMS always wins. A converted figure is shown with `≈` and "approx." and the page says the coordinator confirms the fee and payment currency. If rates are unavailable, fall back to "ask for a quote" rather than guessing.
- **USA landing page (5 Nov 2026, CEO):** `/usa-quran-classes` and its thank-you page show the US admissions number (786) 964-5678 as clickable `tel:+17869645678` links. No "eligible plans" wording; "No card needed" stays; the Weekend exclusion appears once, in the FAQ. Each saved lead triggers exactly one internal email to admissions through Resend (`/api/usa-lead-notification`, recipients from `LEAD_NOTIFICATION_TO`); nothing is emailed to parents. `tests/usa-landing.test.mjs` and `tests/usa-lead-notification.test.mjs` pin this.

- **Lead shifts (11 Nov 2026, owner):** all times PKT (UTC+5). Day shift 08:00–19:59, Night shift 20:00–07:59 (dated by the evening it started). Lead forms never close. Every free-trial request and contact message stores `lead_shift`/`shift_date` from its submission time, set by a database trigger and never editable. At 08:00 and 20:00 PKT (`oqc-lead-shift-handover` pg_cron job) pending leads (trials new/reviewed, messages new/read) pass to the incoming shift's follow-up queue; the hand-over is logged in `lead_shift_handovers` and does not edit lead rows. Rules live in `src/lib/lead-shift.js` and `supabase/migrations/20261111090000_lead_shift_schedule.sql`; `tests/lead-shift.test.mjs` and `tests/lead-shift-db.test.mjs` pin them.
