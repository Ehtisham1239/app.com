# OQC authority, competitor and measurement operating plan

This is an operating checklist, not a record of links won, outreach sent or measured traffic.
No agency costing is included.

## Legitimate authority workflow

Research education/community resources that serve the actual audience, relevant organisations and
existing partnerships the owner can verify. For each opportunity, record the exact URL, audience
relevance, observation date, contact permission, proposed useful resource and outcome in Growth plan.
A published link counts only after checking its destination, context, live status and relationship.
Do not turn a monthly numerical quota into automatic link buying, duplicate submissions or fake
partnerships. No placements or external messages have been made in this implementation.

Prepared message outline for an owner-approved contact:

> Hello [verified contact], your [specific resource] helps [specific audience]. We have published
> [relevant OQC guide] that explains [concrete useful point]. If it would help your readers, you may
> consider it for your resource list. Please let us know if a correction or clarification is needed.

Personalise this only after checking the source. Sending requires the owner’s explicit instruction;
this template is not an outreach action.

## Competitor evidence register

The supplied proposal names EQuran School, TarteeleQuran and Riwaq Al Quran. Its observations are
reference material, not a fresh measurement by this implementation. Start with those three candidates;
discover further competitors from actual USA/Australia query evidence rather than inventing a list
of 8–10 brands to satisfy a count. Record:

- exact observed page and date, country/query/device and the source of the observation;
- intent, useful content/learning information, navigation and conversion journey;
- the relevant OQC canonical target and a concrete audience need to address;
- measured search/backlink data only if authorised data actually supplies it.

The launch closes the proposal’s earlier missing-blog, missing-country-route and fragmented-trial
issues. It does not prove that OQC outranks any competitor or that old competitor fees remain current.

## Search and AI observation

For Search Console, verify the selected production property and export equivalent country-filtered
periods for queries, pages, clicks, impressions and CTR. For GA4, verify the property, consent and
successful non-PII lead-event setup before treating browser events as measured conversions.

For answer engines, record engine/version if visible, exact prompt, market context, observation date,
answer and actual cited URLs. Run the same observations over time and distinguish mention, citation
and referral traffic. A schema change or an AI-friendly paragraph is not evidence of inclusion.

## Six-month operating cadence

Month 1: validate queries, source access, technical baseline and owner-supplied trust details.
Month 2: evaluate the two priority pages against real demand and improve their useful information.
Month 3: review course paths, reading guides, content engagement and enquiries; assess the first checkpoint.
Month 4: refresh internal links and titles using real page/query evidence; continue useful editorial work.
Month 5: review trial usability and permissioned authority work against qualified enquiries.
Month 6: compare equivalent USA/Australia periods, document limitations and choose the next priorities.

Each review needs real elapsed time and source data. The software provides the workspace and records;
it does not manufacture six months of rankings, backlinks or campaign results in a single release.
