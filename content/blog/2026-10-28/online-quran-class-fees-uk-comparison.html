To compare online Quran class fees in the UK, put the complete teaching arrangement beside the price: lesson length, weekly frequency, course, individual or shared format, billing currency and the terms for missed lessons. Then ask what is included and what needs separate confirmation. A monthly headline alone cannot tell you how much teaching time is offered or whether the arrangement suits your learner.

This guide explains how to request a clear comparison. It does not quote an unapproved fee, recommend a financial product or promise a particular learning result. For the college’s current options, use the published packages and ask the coordinator to confirm the applicable GBP amount.

Compare the same kind of lesson

Begin with the learning goal. Reading foundations, supported Quran reading, Hifz, Arabic and translation may require different teaching arrangements. A beginner package should not automatically be compared with a specialist course as though the two offers were identical. Write the actual course name beside each quote.

Confirm whether the lesson is one-to-one or shared and how the scheduled time is used. Online Quran College describes live one-to-one lessons. Ask the coordinator to confirm the course, teacher and package available for your enquiry. If you are unsure about the learning pathway, use the course comparison before comparing fees.

Write down lesson length and weekly frequency

Record how long each lesson lasts and how many lessons are normally arranged in a week. Ask whether the quoted schedule applies to the whole billing period and how holidays or unusual calendar months are handled. Avoid assuming that a monthly plan always means exactly four weeks.

For your own comparison, you can calculate the scheduled minutes in the period described by the provider. For example, multiply the confirmed lesson length by the confirmed number of lessons. That arithmetic helps compare the offer, but it does not measure teaching quality or guarantee that a learner will progress faster with more minutes.

Confirm the currency and final payable amount

Check that the quote is actually in GBP and applies to the package you are considering. A currency label on a website is not, by itself, an approved local fee. If the site asks you to request a quote, obtain that quote rather than converting a number from another country and treating the result as the college’s price.

Ask whether any additional charge applies and when payment is due. If there is a family or promotional offer, confirm its eligibility and duration in writing. Do not assume that an offer seen on another page or mentioned by another family applies to your enrolment.

Understand the free trial separately from the paid package

A trial helps a learner and family review the teaching arrangement before deciding on regular lessons. Use it to discuss the starting level, hear the teacher’s explanation and confirm the proposed timetable. It should help answer practical questions, rather than replace a clear description of the paid package.

Online Quran College’s three-day free trial applies to eligible plans; the Weekend plan is excluded. Confirm which arrangement your request relates to. After the trial, ask for the regular lesson length, frequency, fee currency and applicable terms before choosing whether to enrol.

Check missed-lesson and rescheduling terms

Ask what happens if the learner cannot attend, how much notice is needed and whether a make-up lesson may be offered. Also ask what happens when the teacher is unavailable or a technical problem interrupts the class. Different situations may be covered by different parts of the written policy.

Do not assume that every missed lesson is automatically refunded or rescheduled. Read the current terms and conditions and refund policy, then ask about anything you do not understand. A verbal expectation should not substitute for a policy you can review before paying.

Use UK local time with an actual date

A package is only useful if the offered slot can be maintained. Confirm the lesson date, start time and time zone with the coordinator. Seasonal clock changes can affect the relationship between the learner’s local time and a teacher’s schedule abroad. Avoid relying on a fixed time difference throughout the year.

The UK learning hub includes a dated planner to support the conversation. Use it with your actual proposed date, then obtain confirmation of teacher availability. Country coverage describes online lessons from home, not a claim that a local physical branch or locally based teacher is included in the fee.

Ask what support is included

Discuss how the practice task is provided, how progress is reviewed and whom to contact with a teaching or scheduling question. Ask whether materials are supplied, recommended or obtained separately. These details can affect how well the arrangement fits the learner, even when two headline prices look similar.

If your child needs a particular teaching language or you wish to request a female or male teacher, confirm availability rather than treating a preference as guaranteed. Ask how a change of teacher would be handled. The purpose is a clear agreement, not a promise that every request can be met at every time.

Use a comparison sheet with practical headings

Create a short note for each provider or package with the same headings: course, format, teacher availability, lesson length, weekly schedule, billing period, currency, confirmed amount, missed-lesson terms and next review point. Leave a space for unanswered questions instead of guessing at missing information.

Then identify which differences matter to your learner. A shorter lesson at a manageable time may suit one child better than a longer lesson at a difficult time. Another learner may need a different course or frequency. The lowest headline fee is one fact to consider; it is not a complete assessment of suitability.

Review the trial before increasing the commitment

After the trial, ask the learner what was clear and ask the teacher which starting point they recommend. Compare that recommendation with the proposed package. If the timetable, task size or teaching explanation did not work well, discuss the issue before simply adding more lessons.

Keep the final agreement accessible

Before regular enrolment, keep the confirmed course, schedule, amount, currency and applicable policy links together. Check how to contact the college if circumstances change. A clear record reduces uncertainty and gives you a practical reference if a later question arises.
