# OQC 100-Keyword Market Map — 2026-11-04

## Purpose
A 100-query candidate map for Online Quran College across the five target markets: UK, USA, Canada, Australia and UAE. It is a page-intent and research backlog, **not** a claim that these terms have verified search volume, difficulty, CPC, current positions, or guaranteed traffic.

## Evidence and validation status
- Seed terms are derived from OQC's stated services and target markets: live one-to-one Quran lessons, children/adults, teacher preference, Qaida, Quran reading, Tajweed, Hifz, Arabic and Islamic Studies.
- Search volume, keyword difficulty, CPC, SERP features, competitor share and current rank: **NOT VERIFIED**. Obtain from Google Search Console query exports and a licensed keyword dataset before prioritising by demand.
- Avoid creating five near-duplicate pages per keyword. Each query must map to the strongest existing page; create a new page only where distinct audience intent and useful original content justify it.
- Keep homepage for the global brand/core proposition. Use existing country hubs for country-intent queries. Map course and audience queries to existing relevant pages.
- The 3-day trial qualifier applies only to eligible plans; Weekend plan is excluded. Do not imply every plan is eligible.
- OQC is online-only. Do not create fake city-office pages, addresses, local business listings or location citations.

## Implementation sequence
1. Export 3–16 months of Search Console queries/pages/countries/devices and GA4 landing-page conversions.
2. Join actual impressions, clicks, CTR, average position and qualified trial/enrolment conversions to this map.
3. Prioritise by commercial intent, current impression opportunity, conversion quality, page fit and realistic competition—not by guessed volume.
4. Update one canonical destination per intent cluster: title, H1, introduction, useful supporting sections, internal links, image alt text and CTA. Avoid keyword stuffing.
5. Validate canonical, indexability, sitemap inclusion, rendered content, structured data and mobile UX; then request indexing where appropriate.
6. Monitor weekly and keep a dated change log. Measure qualified trial bookings and paid admissions, not rankings alone.

## Citation and authority programme (manual, evidence-led)
- First-party: keep About, Contact, teaching methodology, safeguarding, teacher verification process, policies, pricing and trial eligibility accurate and consistent.
- Earned editorial: pitch genuinely useful parent guides, Quran-learning resources and expert-reviewed educational contributions to relevant Muslim community, parenting and education publishers. Require editorial relevance and disclosure; no paid followed links.
- Organisation profiles: claim only legitimate profiles where OQC meets the platform's eligibility rules; use consistent legal name, website and contact details. Online-only status means no invented physical location or ineligible Google Business Profile.
- Review platforms: invite genuine customers neutrally; never gate reviews, fabricate testimonials, incentivise positive sentiment or auto-post reviews.
- Link reclamation: seek correction of unlinked brand mentions and broken links where the publisher agrees.
- Track publisher, URL, contact, date, placement, target URL, link attribute, status and evidence in a citation/link register. Do not call a submission complete until the public listing is verified.

## AEO / GEO content standards
- Answer real parent/student questions directly and accurately; include clear definitions, lesson process, assessment and progress feedback.
- Identify real authors/reviewers and their verifiable qualifications. Qur'an/Hadith claims must be checked against reliable sources; disputed or fatwa-level content requires qualified scholar review.
- Add structured data only when it accurately describes visible page content and meets Google policies. Do not invent ratings, reviews, staff credentials, student counts or awards.
- Do not create mass-produced city/keyword doorway pages or artificial AI mentions.
