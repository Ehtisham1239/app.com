# OQC Country, Keyword & Competitor Intelligence Map
**Prepared:** 2026-11-03  
**Scope:** Online Quran College (OQC), global online service; priority markets UK, USA, Canada, Australia and UAE.  
**Purpose:** Page-by-page keyword ownership, competitor monitoring, content-gap planning and safe indexation.

## Operating rules
1. Assign one primary search intent and one primary keyword to each indexable URL. Use close variants naturally in headings, copy, FAQs, internal links and metadata; do not repeat exact phrases unnaturally.
2. Competitor names are for internal research only. Do not insert competitor brands into OQC page copy, schema, testimonials or backlink anchors.
3. Competitor lists below are a SERP-monitoring watchlist, **not a verified ranking order or top-10 ranking claim**. Recheck country-specific Google results monthly and record date, query, device, location and observed position.
4. Never claim a physical branch, local teachers, local accreditation, local phone number, local reviews or local pricing unless OQC can substantiate it. OQC is an online-only academy.
5. Keep thin or templated city/country pages noindex until each has genuinely useful local detail, verified time-zone guidance, market-specific FAQs and clear internal links. Do not bulk-index doorway pages.
6. No invented search volumes, keyword difficulty, rankings, backlinks, review scores, competitor prices or credentials. Connect Search Console/Ahrefs before making numeric opportunity claims.
7. Preserve the 3-day trial wording only for eligible plans; Weekend plan excluded. No-card wording only for eligible plans.

## Internal linking rules
- Country hubs link to relevant courses, trial/pricing, teacher preference, parent guidance and relevant country/time-zone blog posts.
- Course pages link to the matching beginner guide, practice resource and relevant parent/adult article.
- Blogs link contextually to one relevant course and one relevant free-trial or supporting guide; avoid sitewide exact-match anchor repetition.
- Use descriptive, varied anchors. Do not link every page to every country hub.
- Keep canonical URL, sitemap inclusion and robots directives aligned. A noindex page must not be promoted as an indexable target via sitemap.

- Weekly: Search Console queries/pages, clicks, impressions, CTR, position; crawl/index coverage and sitemap errors.
- Monthly: manually verify country SERPs and competitor changes in private/localized sessions; record dated evidence.
- Monthly: review organic landing-page conversion to trial starts, qualified leads, enrolments and retention.
- Quarterly: consolidate overlapping pages, refresh outdated pages, assess whether noindex city pages meet the unique-content threshold.
- Backlinks: pursue relevant editorial, community, education and Muslim-family resources with transparent outreach; no paid link schemes, PBNs or bulk directory spam.
- AEO/GEO: write concise answer-first sections, factual FAQs, clear author/reviewer attribution where verified, source citations for religious claims and machine-readable structured data that matches visible page content.
