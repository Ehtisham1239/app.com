## 1. The one rule that decides eligibility

Online Quran College LTD is a UK company that teaches Australian families online. It has **no Australian address, Australian phone number or ABN**.

- **Do not** use a virtual office, a relative's address or a made-up Australian address or number. Google, Bing and most Australian directories require that the business really operates from the listed address. Fake addresses get listings suspended and can harm the main site's trust. MASTER-OPERATING-SPEC also forbids mass low-quality directory submissions.
- Google Business Profile remains **excluded** unless the owner authorises it separately (MASTER-OPERATING-SPEC). An honest Google Business Profile would carry the UK address and phone anyway, so it would not be an Australian listing.
- If the college later has a **real** Australian presence (an office or registered address, an Australian number, an ABN), Tier 2 below opens up. Update the NAP block once, then submit everywhere with exactly the same details.
