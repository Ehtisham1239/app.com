# OQC 500-keyword map — 5 November 2026

**Purpose:** A controlled keyword-to-canonical-URL research backlog for USA, UK, Canada, Australia and UAE. This file is not intended to be pasted wholesale into page copy, metadata, footers or hidden text.

## Evidence and limits
- Source: connected Google Search Console domain property `sc-domain:onlinequrancollege.com`, last 90 days, retrieved 5 November 2026.
- 79 relevant unique phrases were observed in the five priority markets after excluding unrelated login/portal, competitor, city/near-me and off-target local-intent queries.
- Remaining phrases are semantic candidates, not verified demand.
- GSC impressions, clicks and average positions are not monthly search volumes. Search volume and keyword difficulty are unavailable in this connected report.
- OQC is online-only. Do not create local-office claims, city doorway pages or "near me" copy that implies in-person availability.

## Implementation rules
1. 500 keywords map to existing canonical URLs; they do not mean 500 pages or 500 keyword repetitions on the site.
2. Keep one primary intent per URL. Use the map to improve existing pages, headings, FAQs, useful articles and internal links.
3. Validate candidate terms in country-specific SERPs and Keyword Planner/Ahrefs/Semrush before adding them to visible copy.
4. Reject cannibalising variants and any phrase inconsistent with OQC's actual course or service availability.
5. Measure qualified enquiries, attended trials and paid enrolments, not impressions alone.
