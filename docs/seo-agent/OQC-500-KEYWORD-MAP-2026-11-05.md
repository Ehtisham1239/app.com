# OQC 500-keyword map — 5 November 2026

**Purpose:** A controlled keyword-to-URL research backlog for the five priority markets. This is not a keyword-stuffing list and must not be pasted wholesale into page copy, metadata, footers or hidden text.

## Evidence and limits
- GSC source: connected domain property `sc-domain:onlinequrancollege.com`, last 90 days as retrieved 5 November 2026.
- 121 unique query phrases were observed in the five priority markets in the retrieved report; the map contains 500 unique candidate phrases in total.
- GSC impressions/clicks/average position are included only for matching observed queries. They are not market-wide monthly search volumes.
- Search volume and keyword difficulty are **not available** for this dataset; candidate expansions are explicitly unverified until checked in Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs/Semrush or equivalent market SERPs.
- City/“near me” phrases are not being expanded into fake local-office pages. OQC is online-only; country pages describe online availability, not branches.

## Implementation rules
1. One primary intent per existing canonical URL; do not create 500 pages.
2. Use the map to prioritize page refreshes, headings, FAQs and genuinely useful articles. Add a term to visible copy only where it reads naturally and matches the page.
3. Keep course queries on their course pages; audience queries on the existing kids/adults guides; fees on pricing; transactional trial queries on /free-trial; country-commercial queries on the five country hubs.
4. Validate each keyword in its target country's SERP before writing. Reject cannibalising variants, low-intent and irrelevant queries.
5. Track qualified enquiries and paid enrolments, not just impressions.

## Priority markets
USA, UK, Canada, Australia and UAE. Review GSC by market and canonical URL monthly; fix the highest-value existing page before creating new content.
