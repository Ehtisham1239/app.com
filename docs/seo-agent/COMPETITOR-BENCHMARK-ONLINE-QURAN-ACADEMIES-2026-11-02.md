**Method:** Public website/search-result review. This is a directional snapshot, not a complete market census, independent verification of competitors' claims, or a ranking/market-share study. Prices and offers can change; recheck before using in commercial decisions.

## 2. Patterns visible across the category

1. **Price transparency is becoming table stakes.** Stronger pages explain monthly price alongside class duration, weekly frequency, trial eligibility and sibling/household policy.
2. **The trial is a primary conversion mechanism.** Competitors explain what the learner actually experiences, rather than simply repeating “free trial”.
3. **Parents want evidence of progress.** Portals, lesson notes, assessments, parent feedback and a visible teaching-quality process help reduce uncertainty.
4. **Teacher fit matters.** Female/male preference, language, learner age, subject, qualifications and availability need to be discussed before enrolment.
5. **Country-specific logistics are a real differentiator.** Local clock changes, currency, school routines and contact pathways are more useful than inserting city names into otherwise identical pages.
6. **Interactive utility content is a visible differentiator.** Placement checklists, Hifz planners and fee calculators can solve a real problem and earn references when accurate and maintained.
7. **Competitor claims are not evidence.** “Certified”, “qualified”, active-student totals, completion outcomes, accreditation, “best”, and testimonials must not be copied or treated as verified facts.

## 3. OQC positioning opportunity

OQC should not compete by undercutting every competitor or copying their local phone-number/branch structure. OQC is an online-only academy. The differentiated promise should be:

**A clear, one-to-one Quran learning pathway with parent-aware scheduling, transparent package terms, an understandable trial process and responsible academic feedback.**

The advantage must be demonstrated through real operations:
- A sample (anonymised and consented) progress update or parent report.
- A clear explanation of how the learner's starting point is discussed.
- A real description of what a trial lesson contains.
- Published package frequency, lesson duration, fees and exceptions.
- Actual teacher profiles and qualifications only after the teacher has consented and the credentials are verified.
- A clear parent support and complaints route.
- Academic review for Quran/Tajweed/Arabic/Tafseer content.

## 4. Market-specific execution

### United Kingdom
- Lead with GBP prices, GMT/BST and family scheduling.
- State online-only delivery and avoid local-office/branch language.
- Clarify trial eligibility (the Weekend plan is excluded) and refund/rescheduling terms.
- Build parent and adult-beginner learning pathways.

### United States
- Use city/date-aware time planning across US time zones.
- Show USD pricing and exact package frequency/lesson length.
- Explain parent/guardian-led child enquiries and how a trial is arranged.
- Avoid state licensing or local branch claims.

### Australia
- Use state/city-aware time planning and daylight-saving differences.
- Show AUD fee guidance, with approved fees distinguished from FX estimates.
- Explain how families can choose a repeatable time around school and work.
- Avoid ABN/accreditation/local staffing claims without documentary evidence.

### UAE
- Use Asia/Dubai time and AED quote guidance.
- Explain online-only service and distinguish it from a locally licensed physical institute.
- Avoid trade licence, education permit, UAE office, local staff or local accreditation claims unless documentary evidence exists.
- Answer common questions about lesson frequency, teacher availability, trial terms and payment before asking for a lead.

## 6. Competitive intelligence governance

- Recheck competitor pricing monthly and record the exact URL, date, currency, lesson duration, frequency, trial terms and billing cycle.
- Separate verified public page facts from search snippets and competitor self-claims.
- Do not scrape private systems or create fake reviews, comparison pages, local listings or backlinks.
- Do not write “OQC is better than X” unless a transparent, current and like-for-like comparison supports the statement.
- Use competitor research to find unmet user needs, not to copy headings, wording, testimonials or design.
- Prioritise qualified trial attendance, enrolment, retention and LTV over raw traffic or lead volume.
