# OQC Five-Market Competitor Reverse-Engineering & Implementation Audit
**Research date:** 5 November 2026  
**Markets:** United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Australia, United Arab Emirates  
**Repository:** https://github.com/Ehtisham1239/app.com  
**Canonical site:** https://www.onlinequrancollege.com  
**Purpose:** Translate publicly observable competitor strengths into lawful, original OQC improvements. This is a directional public-web audit, not a verified ranking report or a complete competitor census.

## 1. Executive decision

OQC already has more of the expected technical and content architecture than a surface-level competitor review would suggest:
- One global EducationalOrganization/WebSite JSON-LD graph, canonical metadata, sitemap and robots policy.
- Five indexed market hubs with local currency/time-zone planning and explicit online-only disclosure.
- Course, audience, pricing, FAQ, free-trial and learning-guide routes.
- A defined OQC learning cycle: Learn → Practice → Assessment → Feedback → Improve.
- Teacher cards and permission-based family testimonials on the homepage.
- Article BlogPosting/Breadcrumb schema, internal related-content links and conversion CTAs.
- Existing country-level source/reference sections and an external-citation opportunity register.

Therefore, the strategy is **not** to clone competitors' page layouts, multiply city pages, or add generic schema. The strongest next improvements are evidence-led trust, a clearer trial experience, demonstrable progress reporting, academic review and legitimate editorial authority.

### What competitors visibly do better
1. Make the first three steps of enrolment concrete and easy to understand.
2. Put class duration, weekly frequency, monthly total, currency and trial terms beside one another.
3. Explain parent visibility and progress reporting with a tangible example or portal.
4. Offer diagnostic/utility tools that answer a learner's question before the lead form.
5. Build country-specific schedules and prices into the buying decision, not just the page title.

### What OQC should not imitate
- Unverified student/teacher totals, ratings, accreditation, Ijazah/DBS claims or success rates.
- Local office, school, branch or staff claims in markets where OQC operates online only.
- Fake review markup, fabricated testimonials, keyword-stuffed country pages, paid links or mass directories.
- Competitor text, photographs, code, layouts, downloadable materials or private data.

## 2. Research method and evidence limits

Sources are public competitor pages and search snippets reviewed on 5 November 2026, plus OQC source code, CMS records and dated repository audit reports. A search result's appearance does not establish a competitor's Google position, organic traffic, backlinks, market share or quality. Competitor self-claims are recorded as claims, not independently verified facts. Search snippets may be stale; recheck the source page before a commercial comparison.

No competitor's private analytics, paid SEO metrics, backlink profile, account data or non-public systems were accessed. No competitor content or assets were copied.

## 5. OQC implementation audit

### 5.1 Technical SEO and entity schema

**Confirmed in source**
- Shared layout emits WebSite + EducationalOrganization/Organization JSON-LD with stable entity IDs.
- Canonical metadata, Open Graph, robots and a dynamic sitemap are implemented.
- Five priority markets are included in the organization service-area graph.
- Location pages use Service schema, with provider linked to the Organization entity; this avoids implying a physical branch.
- Blog pages emit BlogPosting and BreadcrumbList data.
- Robots policy explicitly permits OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot and PerplexityBot while protecting private/admin routes. This is crawl permission, not proof of indexing, citation or ranking.

**P0 authenticity correction in this PR**
- CMS `site.identity.established` currently contains 2018 and the shared layout was emitting it as Organization `foundingDate`.
- Companies House directly shows an older same-name company (13629654) incorporated 17 September 2021 and dissolved 20 February 2024. A secondary aggregator reports a separate active same-name company (17075457) incorporated 6 March 2026, but its official Companies House page could not be directly retrieved in this audit.
- This name/record collision means neither date can safely be assumed to describe the CMS brand history or the current contracting entity. Remove the unverified CMS year from JSON-LD until the owner confirms the exact company number and supplies evidence for the brand's 2018 start. Do not add either company's incorporation date or address based on name matching alone.
- Do not add LocalBusiness or a street address while the service remains online-only and no eligible in-person customer-facing location is verified.

**Remaining technical evidence gates**
- Run Rich Results Test / Schema Markup Validator on production after deployment.
- Inspect canonical host, redirects, sitemap and robots responses from a connected browser after the exact SHA is deployed.
- Obtain fresh GSC evidence for indexing and Core Web Vitals; previous lab scores are not field CWV.
- Confirm any old subdomains or Vercel aliases have the intended noindex/redirect behaviour outside this repository.

### 5.2 Content and topical coverage

**Confirmed from repository/CMS audit**
- OQC has existing country hubs, course pages, audience/learning guides, 30+ published articles in the dated CMS audit and an internal link map.
- The 4 November audit checked 12 recently published posts: all had SEO fields, headings, internal links and a free-trial CTA; none of the 12 article bodies contained external references.
- Religious accuracy review remains outstanding for Tajweed, Makharij, Hifz methodology and Tafseer articles. This audit is not a scholar sign-off.

**Gaps and actions**
1. Add authoritative external sources only where an article makes a factual, legal, safety, health, policy or religious claim that needs a source. Do not add citations merely to create outbound links.
2. For Qur'an/Hadith quotations, verify exact Arabic, translation, surah/ayah or hadith collection/reference, grading where relevant, and context; route disputed/fatwa-level issues to a qualified scholar.
3. Add transparent author/reviewer bios only for real contributors who consent and whose role/qualifications are verified.
4. Refresh existing articles before adding more generic “online Quran classes in [city]” posts. Preserve the OQC 1,000-word editorial ceiling where possible; usefulness and completeness take priority over word count.
5. Strengthen Canada and UAE support with unique answers and primary-source references; avoid another generic country keyword page because hubs and supporting articles already exist.

### 5.3 Backlinks, citations and entity authority

**Confirmed / needs owner verification**
- The citation register records current public social/Trustpilot URLs, but account-admin ownership is not verified.
- A public LinkedIn company result for “Online Quran College LTD” surfaced with “Trusted since 2014”, a 5-day trial and 1,000+ student claims. This conflicts with the current site’s 3-day eligible-plan trial and is not in the verified citation register. Treat it as an unverified/possibly stale profile until the owner confirms control and corrects its public facts; do not add it to Organization `sameAs` yet.
- RISE and ISRE resource opportunities are researched; ISLA contribution eligibility is conditional. No outreach or submission is recorded as sent or accepted.
- No acquired backlink is claimed by the reviewed register.
- Google Business Profile and generic location directories are excluded for the online-only model. No false NAP, virtual office or market-specific local phone should be created.

**Legal/quality plan**
- Prioritise one genuinely useful, academically reviewed resource with a clear audience and an original OQC contribution.
- Submit only where the publisher explicitly accepts the resource and after owner approval; disclose OQC affiliation.
- Record prospect, source URL, editorial policy, contact route, approval, date sent, acceptance and final live URL.
- Do not buy links, use PBNs, exchange links for ranking credit, mass-submit directories or send automated spam.
- Treat social profiles and Trustpilot as entity/reputation channels, not as guaranteed ranking levers. Owner must verify account access and business facts before editing.

### 5.4 Conversion and free-trial experience

**Confirmed from current public/source evidence**
- OQC promotes a 3-day trial on eligible plans; Weekend plan is excluded.
- The form collects learner/audience details, country/time zone, teacher preference, contact details and consent.
- The site has a visible trial CTA, pricing information, FAQs, reviews, teacher profiles and country discovery.
- Competitors more explicitly explain what happens in the trial, show progress-report examples and/or provide a placement tool.

**Implementation recommendations**
1. Add a concise trial timeline to the existing trial page; do not create a second trial form.
2. Add a “what you can assess during the trial” checklist: learner starting point, teacher communication, class format, schedule fit and next learning goal. Avoid promising an outcome in three days.
3. Add a progress-report example only after it is anonymised, genuine, consented and approved by Academic/Privacy.
4. Clarify the follow-up owner and realistic response window using actual Admissions SLA data; do not promise 24/7 or a fixed response time without evidence.
5. Test form keyboard/screen-reader usability, error recovery, mobile completion, consent, duplicate submission handling and CRM receipt in an approved test environment.
6. Reconcile CTA events with GA4/GTM and Admissions/CRM records before reporting conversion rates.

### 5.5 AEO/GEO and AI search readiness

**Confirmed**
- The site exposes structured Organization, WebSite, Service, BlogPosting, Breadcrumb and FAQ data where appropriate.
- The robots source explicitly allows major search and named answer-engine crawlers.
- Editorial standards and visible page content provide a foundation for entity clarity and answer extraction.

**Improvements**
- Put a concise direct answer near the top of relevant guides, followed by evidence, context and practical steps.
- Keep claims consistent across About, teachers, pricing, trial, FAQ, schema and third-party profiles.
- Cite primary sources in the page body where claims need evidence; use named author/reviewer identity only when real.
- Make course definitions and trial policies explicit, stable and internally linked.
- Do not add FAQ schema to content that is not visible; do not expect FAQ rich results or AI citations from schema alone.
- Monitor AI referrals/citations only when reliable analytics or source evidence exists. No ranking or citation guarantee is possible.

## 9. Measurement baseline and unknowns

A dated 2 November Search Console snapshot in the repository recorded average positions: UK 25.6, USA 33.2, Australia 44.7, Canada 41.8 and UAE 10.0 (only five UAE impressions). This is a historical 28-day snapshot, not a current ranking report and not comparable to competitor rankings. Competitor positions, keyword volumes, difficulty, organic traffic, referring domains and backlink quality were not independently measured in this audit.

Do not declare “top 1” or “top 3” based on a crawl, a search snippet, a schema change or an AI citation. Track the same query/page/country/device set in GSC over a consistent period, then connect qualified trial and enrolment outcomes.
