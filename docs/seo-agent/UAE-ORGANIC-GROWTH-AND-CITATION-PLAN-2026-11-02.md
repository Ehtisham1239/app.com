# OQC UAE organic growth and citation plan
Updated: 2 November 2026
Market scope: United Arab Emirates (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Fujairah, Umm Al Quwain)

## Positioning and facts
- OQC is an online-only Quran learning provider. UAE pages describe lessons delivered online to families in the UAE; they must not imply a UAE branch, classroom, staffed office, local address, or UAE education-centre licence.
- Currency: AED. Local time: Asia/Dubai (UTC+4).
- Seven emirate/city paths are available under /locations/united-arab-emirates. City pages remain noindex until unique, evidence-backed content and local search demand are verified.
- Do not publish AED package amounts until Finance approves the actual AED price list. Where the website shows a converted guide price, it must remain clearly approximate and the final amount is confirmed by Admissions.

## Citation and entity rules
1. Keep the canonical business identity and contact details consistent with the real OQC legal entity. Do not create a UAE phone, address, branch, licence number, trade name, or local office.
2. Do not create a Google Business Profile for OQC as an online-only business. Google requires in-person customer contact and lists online-only businesses as ineligible. Do not use a virtual office or coworking address to bypass this.
3. Do not submit OQC to UAE government trade registries or emirate chambers as a UAE-registered company unless OQC actually has the relevant UAE legal entity/licence and documentary proof. The UAE National Economic Register is a licence-verification source, not a generic SEO citation directory.
4. Prioritise editorial citations and legitimate international education/Quran learning directories that explicitly accept overseas online providers. Disclose that OQC teaches online and identify its actual legal entity location.
5. Trustpilot is a reputation/review platform, not proof of UAE local presence. Invite all customers neutrally; never filter invitations by sentiment or rating.
6. Record each citation's publisher, exact profile URL, legal-name/NAP fields, eligibility evidence, date checked, owner, and correction/removal route. No bulk submissions or paid links that misrepresent location.

## UAE compliance checkpoints before paid promotion
- Confirm whether the exact service model and contracting entity trigger any UAE education/training licence or emirate-specific approval. Seek written advice from the relevant authority or qualified UAE counsel before representing a local regulated activity.
- Dubai education/training institute marketing can require KHDA approval depending on the licensed provider/activity. Abu Dhabi and other emirates have their own competent authorities; do not assume one emirate's approval covers all.
- Use only appropriately authorised creators/advertisers where the UAE Media Council permit applies. Check the current rules and any exemptions for a company's own services before contracting a creator.
- Marketing calls/messages require applicable consent and Do Not Call Register checks. Retain evidence of opt-in and suppression preferences.
- Use clear AED prices, trial terms, cancellation/refund terms, and no unsupported outcomes or guarantees.

## Measurement
Track UAE separately in Search Console and GA4 using landing page, country, device, source/medium, free-trial start, form submit, qualified lead, trial attended, paid enrolment and retained student. Use CRM country values consistently (United Arab Emirates / UAE / Emirates mapped to UAE). Do not treat impressions, rankings or clicks as enrolments.
Core KPIs: qualified UAE leads, trial booking rate, attended-trial rate, trial-to-paid conversion, cost per qualified lead, revenue per student, retention and refund rate.
Mark every task complete only when the production URL, dated evidence, and owner approval are recorded.
