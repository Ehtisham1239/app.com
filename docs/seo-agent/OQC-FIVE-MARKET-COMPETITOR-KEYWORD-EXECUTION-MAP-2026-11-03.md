## Executive rules
- The lists below are **competitor monitoring sets**, not an objective ranking of the ten best academies. Order is not a quality score. A provider is included where public search results or a market-facing page show relevant online Quran education; re-check availability, offer, and country relevance before publishing any comparison.
- Search volumes, keyword difficulty, CPC and competitor organic traffic are **not verified** in this document. Do not fabricate them. Validate with Google Search Console, Keyword Planner and a reputable SEO suite after account access.
- One primary intent per URL. Do not create near-duplicate doorway pages or mass city pages. Existing country hubs remain the market landing pages; course pages own course intent; the free-trial page owns conversion intent.
- Never publish competitor comparisons or “best academy” claims without documented criteria, date, evidence and fair disclosure.
- OQC is online-only. Do not imply a local branch, office, local teacher presence or local accreditation.

## Priority and execution order
**P1 — commercial country intent:** one primary keyword family per country hub. Improve title/H1/intro, useful market details, proof, FAQs, internal links and one clear trial CTA. Avoid repeating the same paragraph with country names swapped.

**P1 — conversion:** ensure every country hub links to the right course pages and `/free-trial`; explain trial terms, teacher preference, time zones and pricing accurately.

**P2 — course intent:** improve existing Noorani Qaida, Tajweed, Hifz, Quran reading, kids and adults pages; do not create new URLs if a current page already owns the intent.

**P2 — answer-engine readiness:** direct answers, transparent authorship/reviewer, original OQC learning process, accurate citations and visible HTML. Do not add invented schema, FAQ rich-result promises or AI-search “hacks”.

**P3 — city intent:** index only distinct, helpful pages with genuine demand and unique content. No doorway pages or mass city expansion.

**P3 — authority:** relevant editorial resources, education/community partnerships and expert contributions. No paid link schemes, bulk directory submissions, automated outreach or review manipulation.

## Measurement register (populate only from verified sources)
For each keyword/query and landing page, capture:
- Country/device/date range
- GSC impressions, clicks, CTR and average position
- GA4 organic landing sessions and engaged sessions (after analytics validation)
- Trial form starts, valid submissions and backend-matched enquiries
- Qualified enquiries, attended trials and paid enrolments by source
- Ranking/traffic change vs previous comparable period
- Competitor page URL, observed angle, last checked date and evidence URL

Do not label a keyword “high volume”, “low competition” or “easy win” until a dated source and locale are recorded. Prioritise by commercial intent, relevance, existing impressions, conversion fit and ability to create a genuinely better page — not by estimated volume alone.
