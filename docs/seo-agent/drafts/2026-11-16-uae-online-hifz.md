# Online Hifz classes in the UAE: building a memorisation routine

**Short answer:** Online Hifz works best with daily one-to-one lessons at the same Gulf Standard Time slot, a clear split between new memorisation and revision, and a parent or adult learner who protects 20–40 minutes of home practice every day. A learner should already read the Quran fluently with basic Tajweed before starting.

## Is the learner ready?

A teacher will usually check that the learner:

- reads the Quran from the mushaf without spelling out words;
- applies basic Tajweed rules;
- can sit and focus for a full lesson;
- wants to memorise, not only the parent.

If reading is still slow, a few months of [Quran reading with Tajweed](/courses/quran-nazara) first often makes Hifz faster later.

## How lessons are usually structured

Each lesson commonly has three parts. The balance depends on the teacher and the learner. [ACADEMICS REVIEW]

1. **New lesson (sabaq):** a small new portion, recited to the teacher.
2. **Recent revision (sabqi):** the last few days or weeks of memorisation.
3. **Older revision (manzil):** earlier portions, so they are not forgotten.

Revision matters as much as new memorisation. A teacher will slow down new work if revision becomes weak.

## Fitting Hifz into a UAE week

The UAE uses Gulf Standard Time (UTC+4) all year, so the lesson slot stays fixed. Families often choose:

- an early-evening lesson after school and a short rest;
- a morning lesson during school holidays;
- a lighter revision-only day at the weekend.

## Home practice that actually works

- Listen to the new portion several times before the lesson.
- Recite the new portion to a parent or sibling before bedtime.
- Keep a simple notebook or chart of pages memorised and revised.
- Plan for travel and Eid holidays early, so revision continues.

## Tracking progress

Ask your teacher how progress will be recorded, for example pages or juz completed and revision quality, and how often you will receive an update. Clear records help parents support the routine at home.
