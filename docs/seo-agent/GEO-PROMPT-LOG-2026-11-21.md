## What the results say

1. **Brand entity is weak outside Google.** Answer engines that draw on Bing or other indexes cannot name OQC when asked for it by name. Fixes that need the owner: Bing Webmaster Tools URL submission and IndexNow check (Month 1 item), Trustpilot reviews (E-13), and the citation profiles in CITATION-PACK-2026-11-03.md (E-24).
2. **Competitors win with many third-party mentions**, several of them paid press releases or forum posts. OQC must not copy those (spam policy); the route is the editorial queue in AUTHORITY-PROGRAMME.md.
3. **On-site answers are already in place** (hub FAQs for kids, Hifz, fees and Tafseer; `/llms.txt`, `/llms-full.txt`, `/at-a-glance`; Organization and FAQPage JSON-LD). The gap is off-site trust, not missing on-page text.
