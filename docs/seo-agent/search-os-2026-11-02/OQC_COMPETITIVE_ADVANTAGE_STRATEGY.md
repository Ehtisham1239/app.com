1. **Strongest competitors?** By breadth of country pages: Ijaazah and Surah Institute (USA/Canada), Kalimah Center (Australia/UK), TarteeleQuran (separate country domains incl. .ae). In UAE, tutor marketplaces.
2. **Who dominates each market?** See `OQC_COUNTRY_COMPETITOR_REPORT.md`: UK mixed directories/review pages; USA & Canada Ijaazah/Surah Institute; Australia Kalimah; UAE MyPrivateTutor-type marketplaces.

4. **What creates their visibility?** Dedicated "online Quran classes in {country}" pages plus supporting guides (Ijaazah has 5+ Canada guides) and directory/review-site presence.
