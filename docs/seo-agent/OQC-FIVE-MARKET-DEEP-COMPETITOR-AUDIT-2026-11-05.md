# OQC Five-Market Deep Competitor Reverse-Engineering Audit
**Research date:** 5 November 2026  
**Markets:** United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Australia, United Arab Emirates  
**Scope:** Technical SEO, content, backlinks/entity citations, conversion, AI-search readiness  
**Status:** Public-web competitor audit + repository/CMS implementation review. Not a ranking report, traffic estimate, backlink-count audit or legal review.

## 1. Executive finding

OQC already has a stronger implementation baseline than a generic academy brochure: five country hubs, local-currency pricing logic, multi-zone scheduling, live course/teacher/testimonial data, a structured free-trial form, visible online-only disclosure, FAQ/service/breadcrumb/organization schema, editorial standards, article answer blocks, a crawlable sitemap and AI search crawler access.

The competitor research does **not** justify rebuilding those foundations. The defensible opportunity is to make the operational proof more visible and easier to verify:
1. Show what a parent receives as progress feedback, using a real anonymised example only after academic/parent consent.
2. Explain the trial lesson sequence in concrete terms and align the copy with the actual Admissions workflow.
3. Make teacher verification/qualification evidence transparent at individual-profile level, never blanket claims.
4. Build useful country-specific comparison/help resources with dated methodology and source citations, not thin city pages or competitor-copy pages.
5. Earn a small number of relevant editorial/resource references through genuine educational contribution, not directory volume or paid ranking links.
6. Measure trial quality through valid enquiries, attended trials, paid enrolments and retention—not search traffic alone.

## 2. Research method and limits

- Reviewed publicly accessible competitor landing pages and search result content on 5 November 2026.
- Reviewed the OQC source tree, five-market execution map, country landing components, market-guide data, trial form, homepage, blog template, blog audit and citation tracker.
- Competitor prices/features are self-published and may change; they are not independently verified. They are directional examples only.
- Search snippets are not proof of organic ranking position, traffic, conversion rate, domain authority or backlink count.
- The existing OQC five-market keyword map remains the query-to-URL baseline. No search-volume, KD, CPC or ranking claims are added without authenticated tool data.

## 3. Market-by-market competitor reverse engineering

### United Kingdom

**Observed examples**
- UK Quran Academy: free 3-day trial, one-to-one tuition, weekly parent progress logs, tutor matching, a digital Qaida app and GBP package presentation. Public page: https://ukquranacademy.com/
- Almuallim: one-to-one UK-time positioning, published GBP plans, course pathway, teacher qualification claims and city references. Public page: https://almuallim.uk/
- Online Quran Tuition: free trial, monthly assessment, progress reporting and explicit parent/teacher-fit information. Public page: https://onlinequrantuition.co.uk/online-quran-academy/
- Learn Quran Academy: previously observed trial, student portal, monthly assessment and parent/student testimonials. Public page: https://learnquraan.co.uk/

**What appears to help the competitor experience**
- Parents can quickly understand the weekly learning rhythm, how feedback is delivered and whether a teacher is a fit.
- GBP, lesson frequency and trial conditions are visible near the decision point.
- UK-specific time and family routines are explained rather than relying on a generic “flexible timing” statement.

**OQC comparison**
- Already present: UK country hub, GBP-aware fee presentation, GMT/BST guidance, course links, trial eligibility explanation, teacher preference and online-only disclosure.
- Gap to validate with Admissions/Academic: a concrete, accurate explanation of the progress-review process and an approved sample of a parent update. Do not promise weekly/monthly reports unless that cadence is operationally standard.
- Keep existing UK hub as the primary country-intent URL; do not add city doorway pages.

### United States

**Observed examples**
- Ease Quran Academy: describes teacher checks (certificate review, recitation test, demo lesson, identity/reference checks), US scheduling, free first class, course/pricing choices and parent questions. Public page: https://easequran.com/
- Huda Quran Academy: one-to-one delivery, 3-day trial, weekly tests, monthly parent progress reports and course pathways. Public page: https://hudaquranacademy.com/
- Online Quran Academy USA: informational comparison content targeting Canadian/US academy selection queries. Public page: https://onlinequranacademy.us/

**What appears to help the competitor experience**
- Trust is explained as a process rather than a generic “qualified teachers” badge.
- US time-zone and parent schedule questions are handled early.
- Informational comparison content captures evaluation-stage searches, although self-published “top 10” lists need transparent criteria and conflict disclosure.

**OQC comparison**
- Already present: US country hub, multi-zone scheduling, local currency support, course and teacher data, parent/guardian messaging, trial form and privacy-oriented copy.
- Gap: expose OQC's actual teacher onboarding/verification steps only after HR supplies the written, current process and evidence. Avoid copying competitor checklists as if OQC uses the same process.
- Content opportunity: a dated, criteria-based “How to compare online Quran classes for a child” guide with an original checklist and clear OQC disclosure; not an OQC-authored fake independent ranking.

### Canada

**Observed examples**
- Nouf Quran Academy: Canada-specific page, Eastern/Central/Mountain/Pacific time coverage, CAD billing, course chooser and rescheduling information. Public page: https://www.noufquranacademy.com/online-quran-classes/canada
- Her Quran Academy: online-only disclosure, local time-zone scheduling, CAD fees, family discounts and a time finder. Public page: https://herquranacademy.com/online-quran-classes-canada/
- Canadian Quran Academy: packages presented by lesson length and weekly frequency. Public page: https://canadianquranacademy.com/pricing/

**What appears to help the competitor experience**
- Multi-zone coverage is made tangible; parents can distinguish local time from the tutor's operating time.
- Price comparisons include lesson duration and weekly frequency.
- Online-only status is explicitly stated, avoiding a false impression of local premises.

**OQC comparison**
- Already present: Canadian hub, CAD-aware pricing, city/time-zone planner, online-only disclosure and trial flow.
- Gap: check the fee copy against the exact live plan frequency, lesson duration and payment terms. A local-currency display is not a guarantee that every plan is billed in CAD.
- Add city-specific content only where it helps a parent solve a genuinely different time-zone or scheduling problem.

### Australia

**Observed examples**
- OnlineQuran.pk Australia: AUD packages, AEST/ACST/AWST references, trial and claims of parent progress updates. Public page: https://onlinequran.pk/australia/
- Australia Quran Academy: AUD package tiers, lesson frequency/duration, sibling discounts and pay-as-you-learn terms. Public page: https://australia-quran.academy/pricing/
- Nouf Quran Academy: Australia local-time and currency positioning within its multi-country page. Public page: https://www.noufquranacademy.com/online-quran-classes

**What appears to help the competitor experience**
- Australia needs more than “AEST”: Adelaide, Perth and daylight-saving states differ.
- Parents see the duration and frequency attached to the fee.
- Weekends and after-school scheduling are described as real operating windows.

**OQC comparison**
- Already present: AUD market hub, city-aware IANA time zones, local-fee handling and a country guide.
- Gap: ensure every published example of a lesson time is date-aware and not a static PKT conversion. Re-check DST boundaries and confirm actual teacher availability before promising slots.
- Do not publish “priority scheduling”, guaranteed weekend slots or progress-report cadence unless operations can evidence them.

### United Arab Emirates

**Observed examples**
- Her Quran Academy UAE: online-only statement, Gulf Standard Time, AED packages, after-school/weekend scheduling and no UAE physical centre. Public page: https://herquranacademy.com/online-quran-classes-uae/
- Salam Campus: UAE landing page, parent portal claim, 30-minute free class, GST scheduling, pause/cancel terms. Public page: https://salamcampus.com/online-quran-classes-uae/
- Al Najam Academy: UAE city coverage, teacher preference, AED price claims and trial information. Public page: https://alnajamonlinequranacademy.com/online-quran-classes-in-uae/
- Khan Quran Academy: multi-currency fee matrix including AED, USD, GBP, CAD and AUD. Public page: https://khanquranacademy.com/fee

**What appears to help the competitor experience**
- GST/UTC+4 is easy to understand and does not require a seasonal clock-change explanation for UAE-local schedules.
- AED price and the exact trial terms are visible.
- Expat families value language, teacher preference and family scheduling clarity.

**OQC comparison**
- Already present: UAE hub, AED-aware pricing, Asia/Dubai time zone, city routes, online-only disclosure, courses and trial entry point.
- Gap: verify that Arabic/English/Urdu teaching-language options are explicitly tied to real teacher availability before advertising them.
- Never add a UAE office, local licence, local telephone number or LocalBusiness address without documentary evidence and actual eligibility.

### OQC CMS / product evidence check (5 November 2026)
A read-only Supabase inspection confirms the parent portal is implemented and its route is private/noindex. The current data snapshot contained **2 issued certificates**, **0 monthly progress reports**, and **0 published student-result rows**. The portal can display only records linked to the authenticated parent and published by staff. Accordingly, the country-hub CTA must describe approved results/certificates as available only when published and must not imply that monthly reports are already being delivered to every family. The copy in this PR now makes that limitation explicit.

The homepage CMS also contains the phrase “track progress every month” and a benefit describing certificates after final assessments. The first statement needs confirmation from Academic Operations against the actual reporting cadence before it is amplified in public copy. This audit does not silently rewrite it because monthly teacher feedback may occur outside the portal database. Teacher profile records currently contain qualification text, but this database check does not inspect documentary credential evidence; no blanket credential verification claim is made.

## 5. OQC implementation audit by requested scope

### A. Technical SEO

**Existing implementation evidence in repository**
- Shared Organization/WebSite graph, stable entity IDs and contact data; country pages emit Service, FAQ and Breadcrumb schemas; blog pages emit BlogPosting and Breadcrumb schema.
- Sitemap and robots modules exist; sitemap includes eligible location/course/blog/teacher content.
- Canonical, hreflang and country-market metadata are generated through shared helpers.
- robots rules explicitly allow relevant search and AI search crawlers, including OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot and PerplexityBot, while blocking private areas.
- Prior dated technical audit recorded a 76-URL sitemap crawl with no findings and selected Lighthouse sample pages in the 98–100 performance range. Those are historical lab/sample results, not current whole-site field Core Web Vitals.

**Actions**
1. Re-run sitemap crawl, redirect/canonical/hreflang checks and mobile trial journey on the new release.
2. Inspect structured data on homepage, each of the five hubs, trial, teacher and article routes.
3. Check Search Console URL Inspection and sitemap status only after owner-authorized live property access.
4. Keep the organization schema founding date unset until the brand-start date and legal-company incorporation are independently documented.

### B. Content and on-page relevance

**Existing implementation**
- Five indexed country hubs and existing course/teacher/blog routes.
- Country guides contain market-specific timezone, privacy, consent and consumer-information references.
- Article template shows author/date, editorial standards link, visible article content, internal resources and CTA.
- The 4 November blog audit recorded 32 published posts plus 3 in review; 12 newer posts were audited and all were under the 1,000-word editorial limit. That audit did not certify Google indexing or religious correctness.

**Actions**
1. Prioritise 5 decision-support assets: parent checklist, trial lesson walkthrough, progress-report explainer, teacher verification explainer and five-market fee comparison methodology.
2. Do not create five near-identical country blog posts. Put country-specific logistics in the existing hubs and genuinely distinct details in the appropriate course/guide.
3. Add external citations only where a factual/legal/technical claim needs support; use primary sources and show publication/update dates.
4. Route Tajweed, Makharij, Hifz methodology, Quranic examples and Tafseer content to Academic review; Mufti/scholar review for disputed/fatwa-level content.

### C. Backlinks, citations and entity trust

**Verified from existing tracker**
- Website-linked Facebook, Instagram, X and Trustpilot profiles are recorded, but profile admin ownership was not verified in the tracker.
- Google Search Console is recorded as previously connected but was not freshly re-authorized in the current audit.
- Bing Webmaster, Apple Business brand presence and LinkedIn are owner-verification tasks.
- RISE, ISRE and ISLA opportunities are researched/prepared but not submitted.
- Google Business Profile and physical-address directories are excluded because OQC is online-only and has no eligible in-person customer-contact premises.

**Actions**
1. Owner verifies official profiles and legal entity details; use identical legal name, public contact and website where appropriate.
2. Submit original, academically reviewed learning resources to relevant education/community resource pages only after editorial acceptance criteria are checked.
3. Seek genuine expert contributions and partnerships; allow publishers to choose link qualification.
4. Do not buy followed links, bulk-submit directories, exchange links at scale, automate forum/profile links, or gate reviews.

### D. Conversion and enrolment journey

**Existing implementation**
- Country hubs have fee answer, local-time scheduling, courses, teacher/testimonial modules and direct trial links.
- Trial form collects learner name, age band, country, timezone, course (optional), teacher preference, WhatsApp number, email and consent; marketing opt-in defaults false.
- Form records attribution/UTM fields and posts through the public Supabase RPC; it has a success state and fallback WhatsApp route.
- Trial FAQ states 3 days on eligible plans and excludes the Weekend plan.

**Actions**
1. Validate every country/plan combination against current Supabase fee and trial-eligibility settings.
2. Measure form starts → valid submits → coordinator contact → trial booked → trial attended → paid enrolment. Do not count a click as an admission.
3. Verify GA4/GTM Realtime/DebugView and server-side lead reconciliation; configured IDs alone are not proof of event delivery.
4. Get Admissions to confirm response SLA before publishing “usually within 24 hours” or any faster promise.
5. Add parent progress/report proof only after academic/parent approval; never fabricate testimonials or outcomes.

### E. AI search / AEO / GEO

**Existing implementation**
- Crawlable HTML content, direct FAQ answers, article quick-answer blocks, author/date/editorial standards, Organization/WebSite/Service/BlogPosting/Breadcrumb schema and AI search crawler allow rules.
- Official references are visible on market hubs; source links are relevant to their respective local guidance.

**Actions**
1. Make answers specific and evidence-led; use concise definitions, steps, limits and sources where helpful.
2. Identify author/reviewer and review date on sensitive academic guidance only when the named person actually reviewed it.
3. Keep structured data consistent with visible page content; no invented Review/AggregateRating, local address, accreditation or course outcomes.
4. Track AI referrals/citations where measurable; do not promise inclusion in AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity or other answer engines.

## 7. Release and measurement gates

**PR scope**
- The companion Organization schema correction removes an unverified CMS founding year from JSON-LD.
- This audit document records competitor findings and the implementation backlog; it does not claim unperformed outreach, rankings or third-party account verification.

**Tests required**
- Automated tests, lint and production build must pass.
- Responsive and live-performance/sitemap audit jobs must complete or be explicitly recorded as blocked/skipped with reason.
- Preview route checks must cover the five country hubs and /free-trial.
- Production smoke must pass after deployment.
- Rich Results Test and Search Console URL Inspection remain external validation steps.

**Measurement**
- GSC: query/page/country/device impressions, clicks, CTR and average position.
- GA4: organic landing sessions and valid trial submit events after event verification.
- CRM/Admissions: qualified enquiry, contact attempt, trial booked, trial attended, paid enrolment and retention.
- Report market and cohort separately; do not infer traffic or conversion from competitor snippets.

## 9. Final audit conclusion

OQC should win on verifiable teaching process, clear trial experience, transparent fees, parent communication, academically reviewed content and reliable technical delivery—not on copied competitor claims, fake local presence, fake reviews or purchased links.

No competitor page proves its own claimed student count, credentials, ratings or outcomes. No search result proves a competitor's ranking advantage or backlink profile. Those require independent tools/data and should not be invented.

**Owner-dependent blockers:** authenticated GSC/GA4 access, verified social/profile admin access, documented teacher-verification process, approved parent progress sample, and academic review/approval for religiously sensitive resources.
