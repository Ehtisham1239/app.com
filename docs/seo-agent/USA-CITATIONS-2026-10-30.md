## 1. Eligibility rule (same as Australia)

Online Quran College LTD is a UK company that teaches US families online. It has **no US address or US phone number**.

- **Do not** use a virtual office, mailbox, relative's address or a made-up US number. Google, Bing and Yelp require that a business genuinely operates from the location it lists, and suspended listings harm trust.
- Google Business Profile stays **excluded** unless the owner authorises it separately (MASTER-OPERATING-SPEC).
- BBB accreditation standards cover businesses in North America, so a UK company is outside the programme.
- If a **real** US presence exists later (a registered US entity or office, a US number), Tier 2 opens. Update the NAP block once, then use identical details everywhere.
