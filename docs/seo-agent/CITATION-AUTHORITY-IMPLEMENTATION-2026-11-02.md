Citations do not guarantee Google rankings and cannot replace useful pages, technical SEO, independent editorial coverage, and real customer experience. Do not optimize for raw directory count.

Measure:
- Non-brand organic clicks and impressions by approved country in Google Search Console.
- Organic sessions to country/course pages in GA4.
- Free-trial starts, qualified trial bookings, enrolments and trial-to-paid rate from the admissions/ERP source of truth.
- Referral sessions and qualified leads by external profile.
- Zero conflicting addresses, phone numbers, legal names, or unsupported claims.

## 3. Citation quality rules
1. One genuine OQC-owned profile per relevant platform; claim duplicates only when ownership can be verified.
2. Use the exact brand name and canonical HTTPS URL. Do not create country-specific variants unless separately registered brands genuinely exist.
3. Never use fake addresses, local phone numbers, employees, testimonials, reviews, certifications, or country branches.
4. No mass directory blasts, automated submissions, link farms, paid do-follow links, profile-link schemes, forum signatures, Web 2.0 properties, or keyword-stuffed descriptions.
5. A profile is complete only when live, verified/claimed where possible, fact-checked, and its public URL is recorded.
6. If a platform requires a physical address, local service presence, registration, or proof OQC cannot provide, mark Not eligible / Do not submit.
7. Never buy reviews or gate review requests. Invite genuine parents/adult learners neutrally and request honest feedback without incentives.
8. Use UTM tags where supported. Keep landing pages relevant and preserve consent/privacy requirements.
9. Record submission date, account owner, verification method/status, public URL, exact business data, evidence screenshot, next review date, and platform policy.
10. Prioritize relevance, audience trust, referral potential and editorial independence over domain metrics.
