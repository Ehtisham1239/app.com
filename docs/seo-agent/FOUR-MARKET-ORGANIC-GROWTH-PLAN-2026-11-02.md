# OQC Four-Market Organic Growth & Trust Operating Plan
Updated: 2 November 2026
Markets: United Kingdom (primary), United States (growth), Canada (expansion), Australia (expansion)
Website: https://www.onlinequrancollege.com
Business model: Online-only Quran learning provider; lessons delivered remotely to families and adult learners.

## 1. Business truth and positioning
- OQC is an online-only service. Do not imply a staffed branch, classroom, local office, local telephone number, local registration, or local education licence in any of these four countries.
- The website's actual legal identity and contact details must remain consistent. The existing site identity lists Online Quran College LTD and UK contact details; Finance/Legal must confirm the exact contracting entity, registered office, payment recipient and any VAT/GST/sales-tax obligations before country-specific legal statements are published.
- Do not fabricate student counts, teacher credentials, exam outcomes, reviews, ratings, parent stories, accreditations or conversion claims.
- Do not create country Google Business Profiles for an online-only provider. Do not use virtual offices, mailbox addresses or third-party premises to simulate in-person customer contact.
- Country currency labels are display/localisation only: GBP, USD, CAD, AUD. Do not invent local prices or exchange rates. Finance approves each price list; Admissions confirms the final written quote, lesson frequency, duration, availability, cancellation and refund terms.
- A free trial is an enquiry/experience, not a guarantee of a specific teacher, time, outcome or enrolment.

## 2. Market priority and conversion strategy
| Market | Priority | Currency | Core approach |
| UK | Primary | GBP | Strengthen current legal-entity trust, parent enquiries, local-time scheduling and course-to-trial conversion |
| USA | Growth | USD | City/time-zone clarity, parent-led child enquiries, course education and qualified trial acquisition |
| Canada | Expansion | CAD | Province/city time-zone accuracy, CASL-safe follow-up, meaningful privacy consent |
| Australia | Expansion | AUD | State-specific time planning, transparent online-service terms and ACL-aware consumer communication |

Funnel for every market:
Search impression -> qualified landing visit -> course/teacher/pricing exploration -> trial form start -> consented lead -> Admissions contact -> trial scheduled -> trial attended -> paid enrolment -> 30/60/90-day retention.
Do not report traffic as revenue. Use CRM-confirmed country and currency, and reconcile leads to trial attendance, payment and retention.

## 3. Country page architecture and indexation
Existing canonical country hubs:
- /locations/united-kingdom (en_GB, GBP, Europe/London)
- /locations/united-states (en_US, USD, America/New_York; city-specific zones)
- /locations/canada (en_CA, CAD, America/Toronto; city/province-specific zones)
- /locations/australia (en_AU, AUD, Australia/Sydney; city/state-specific zones)

Keep one country hub per market. City pages remain noindex until each has distinct useful information, a real local scheduling use case, Search Console evidence and manual approval. Do not create thin doorway pages or near-duplicate pages for every suburb.
Keep self-referencing canonical URLs on country hubs. Keep the sitemap limited to approved indexable country hubs. Do not add hreflang tags until all four country pages are published, self-canonical, indexable, language-consistent and cross-linked as a valid equivalent set. If language variants do not exist, do not manufacture language alternates.
Add contextual internal links from relevant course pages, parent guides, pricing, teachers, FAQs and blog posts to the four country hubs and free-trial form. Do not stuff city names into every page.

## 5. AEO and GEO answer design
Each country hub should directly answer, in visible HTML:
1. Is OQC online or in person? Online, live lessons; no local branch is claimed.
2. Who can enquire? Parents/guardians for child learners and adults for themselves.
3. Which courses? Only courses currently published and deliverable by OQC.
4. How does a trial work? Explain actual trial duration, conditions, scheduling and any exclusions exactly as approved.
5. What does it cost? Display a Finance-approved country price or clearly say request a written quote; never imply a price that is not configured.
6. Which time zone? Use the city-specific IANA zone and date-aware conversion.
7. Which teacher can be requested? Male/female preference may be requested; availability is confirmed by Admissions.
8. How are privacy and safeguarding handled? Link the actual current privacy, safeguarding and complaint policies.
9. What happens after the trial? Explain the verified Admissions process, payment terms and cancellation route.

Use concise question-and-answer headings, factual paragraphs, descriptive internal links and schema only when the visible page supports it. Do not add fabricated FAQ schema, ratings, Review snippets, LocalBusiness, School or Course rich-result markup unsupported by page facts. AEO/GEO does not justify publishing invented statistics or unsupported expert credentials.

## 6. Citation and entity-building strategy
A citation is not a link-building target by itself. Only create a profile where the publisher accepts an overseas, online-only service and the listing can accurately state the real legal entity, country of establishment and remote service model.

### UK
- Verify legal identity and registered office against the relevant UK Companies House record before using it in any profile. Never invent a Companies House number.
- Prioritise genuine UK education/community/parenting/Quran-learning publishers only where they explicitly accept online overseas providers and editorial inclusion is justified.
- Do not create a school, tuition-centre, campus or local office listing.
- Keep GBP quotations and consumer terms transparent; confirm whether UK consumer, distance-contract, privacy and tax obligations apply to the actual contracting entity.
- Official references: GOV.UK clock changes; ICO PECR/direct marketing and child data; ICO cookies; ASA/CAP testimonial substantiation.

### USA
- Use only genuine national or state-level education/community resources that accept remote providers and accurately disclose the actual foreign legal entity.
- Do not claim US incorporation, state school registration, local teacher licensing or physical premises without documentary proof.
- Do not create a Google Business Profile for an online-only provider.
- Review COPPA applicability for any personal information collected directly from children under 13; use parent/guardian-led forms, data minimisation and legally required verifiable parental consent.
- Commercial email must comply with CAN-SPAM, including accurate sender/subject details, valid postal address, opt-out and prompt suppression.
- Official references: FTC COPPA FAQ; FTC CAN-SPAM compliance guide; US DOT daylight-saving guidance.

### Canada
- Use legitimate Canadian Muslim community, parent, education and Quran-learning publishers only where their rules accept international remote providers.
- Do not claim Canadian incorporation, provincial school approval, local address or Canadian staff unless verified.
- Record and prove consent for commercial electronic messages to Canadian recipients; identify the sender and include a functioning unsubscribe mechanism under CASL.
- Review PIPEDA and applicable provincial privacy laws. Use parent/guardian consent where the child cannot meaningfully consent; assess provincial variation and the child's maturity.
- Official references: CRTC CASL Act/FAQ; Office of the Privacy Commissioner meaningful-consent guidance and children's privacy code; NRC time-zone guidance.

### Australia
- Use genuine Australian Muslim/community/education publishers that accept online overseas services; disclose the real contracting entity and remote delivery.
- Do not claim an ABN, Australian company, campus, local phone or Australian teacher accreditation unless verified.
- Overseas providers directly offering services to Australian consumers may be subject to Australian Consumer Law. Keep service descriptions, trial terms, pricing, cancellation and refund information accurate and clear.
- Obtain and retain consent for commercial email/SMS, identify the sender and make unsubscribe easy; process unsubscribe requests within the statutory period.
- Official references: ACMA Spam Act guidance; ACCC false/misleading claims and online reviews; ACCC online purchasing; Fair Work daylight-saving guidance.

### Citation register fields
Publisher; exact profile URL; country; directory type; eligibility proof; accepted business model; legal name used; address/phone fields (only real details); description; link attribute; fee; date submitted; approval evidence; next review date; correction/removal contact; owner; status. A listing is not complete until publicly verified and checked for accuracy.

## 8. Analytics and CRM measurement
GSC:
- Verify domain property for onlinequrancollege.com; submit the current sitemap; inspect each of the four canonical hubs; monitor indexing, country-page queries, CTR, clicks and coverage.
- GSC country filters are not a substitute for user-country analytics. Query data may be anonymised and position is not a business outcome.

GA4/GTM:
- Confirm the real production Measurement ID and GTM container before release. Existing default settings contain G-57TGPTDNT4 and GTM-W7CMZS53; validate ownership and actual tag firing before relying on them.
- Events: free_trial_view, form_start, generate_lead, whatsapp_click, contact_click, trial_booked, trial_attended, enrolment_paid, refund_requested.
- Parameters: market_code (UK/USA/CAN/AUS), landing_path, currency, device_category, source/medium, campaign, consent_state. Never send names, email, phone, child details or other PII to GA4.
- Only fire analytics/advertising tags after the applicable consent decision. Implement a consent mechanism that supports relevant UK/EEA requirements before non-essential storage/access and check the rules for other markets.
- Use UTM naming: utm_source / utm_medium / utm_campaign / utm_content; never place personal data in URLs.
- CRM country values: United Kingdom, United States, Canada, Australia; map synonyms once. Currency: GBP, USD, CAD, AUD.

Core KPIs:
Qualified leads; lead-to-trial booked; booked-to-attended; attended-to-paid; cost per qualified lead; cost per attended trial; revenue per enrolled student; 30/60/90-day retention; refund/cancellation; country-specific contribution margin.
Do not set numeric targets until a baseline exists. Compare cohorts by country and channel, not just top-line sessions.

## 9. Paid and organic promotion safeguards
- Start with organic search, parent education content, relevant community partnerships and referral requests to existing families.
- Paid search/social tests require approved country price, conversion tracking, landing-page proof, audience/consent review, budget cap and stop-loss rule.
- Do not target children directly or use children's behavioural data for retargeting. Target parents/adults with age-appropriate, factual messaging.
- No fake urgency, fabricated scarcity, guaranteed fluency/Hifz outcomes, guaranteed teacher slots, fabricated ratings or misleading “local” claims.
- Review platform policies and applicable law before every campaign launch. Keep creative, audience, consent, landing page, spend, leads, attended trials and paid enrolments in one campaign log.

## 10. Technical and quality release gates
- Country hubs return HTTP 200; canonical self-references are correct; only approved hubs are indexable and in sitemap.
- No accidental city-page indexation; no duplicate country hubs; no broken internal links.
- Currency and local-time labels match the correct market; city clocks use IANA zones and DST-aware dates.
- Mobile free-trial form works; validation and consent are understandable; keyboard/accessibility and error states are checked.
- Consent choices block non-essential tags until allowed; analytics events do not contain PII.
- Review claims, teacher bios, certificates, student numbers and testimonials against source evidence and permissions.
- Performance: run Lighthouse/PageSpeed on each country hub and free-trial page, record mobile LCP/INP/CLS, fix critical regressions.
- Release only after production deployment READY, smoke tests and evidence URLs are recorded.
